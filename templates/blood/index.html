Donor Eligibility & Quick Facts

Basic requirements to ensure safe blood transfusion for everyone

Age Requirement

Donors must be between 18 and 65 years old in sound general health.

Healthy Weight

Minimum body weight of 50 kg (110 lbs) is required for donor safety.

Donation Gap

Wait at least 3 months (90 days) between successive whole blood donations.

Safe Transfusion

All blood units undergo routine testing for infectious diseases prior to approval.
